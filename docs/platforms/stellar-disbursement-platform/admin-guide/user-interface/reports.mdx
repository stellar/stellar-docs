The Reports page provides PDF account statements and individual transaction notices.

Reports are off by default. An Owner turns them on with Enable Reports on the Settings page; until then the Reports tab is hidden.

![Settings Reports Toggle](/assets/SDP/SDP51.png)

Once enabled, the page is available to the Owner, Financial Controller, Business, Initiator and Approver roles. Users can only produce reports for distribution accounts and payments they have access to.

![Reports](/assets/SDP/SDP52.png)

The Reports page includes:

- Account Statement: A statement for one distribution account over a period. It is not available for Circle accounts or for organizations on the shared host distribution account.
  - Account: The statement covers the account selected in the [account picker](/docs/platforms/stellar-disbursement-platform/admin-guide/user-interface/account-picker). If "All accounts" is selected, you choose the account in the card instead. Archived accounts can also be selected.
  - Period: This month, Last month, QTD or YTD, or a custom range set with From date and To date (both inclusive).
  - Download Statement: Saves a PDF named after the account and the period, for example `statement_main_20261001-20261031.pdf`.
- Individual Transaction Notice: A notice for a single payment.
  - Search: Search by a full or partial SDP transaction ID or payment ID. Payments are searched within the distribution account(s) selected in the account picker.
  - Results: Transaction ID is the SDP's ID for the payment, and Payment ID is the ID your organization gave it in the disbursement file.
  - Notes: Optional text, up to 500 characters, printed in a marked box on the PDF.
  - Download Notice: Saves the notice as `transaction_notice_<payment id>.pdf`.
