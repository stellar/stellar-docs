---
title: Registration
---

The registration endpoints guide the process for a receiver to verify their identity and link their wallet address to an SDP. The registration process only needs to happen once per receiver to link their wallet. Only SDP-compatible wallet providers can facilitate the registration process. These endpoints must be supported and hit by the wallet providers after the receiver gets the initial invite. After the wallet address is successfully linked, the payment automatically begins.

There are two parts to the registration flow. First, the wallet must authenticate with SEP-10 and initiate a registration flow with SEP-24, using the `/sep10/auth` and `/sep24` endpoints defined below. The Stellar Disbursement Platform hosts these endpoints itself, and its `stellar.toml` file gives their URLs. SDP versions before 6.0.0 hosted them on a separate Anchor Platform host.

The second part of the registration flow is handled by the webview that is opened within the wallet application. This webview uses the `/sep24-interactive-deposit` endpoints defined below to complete the registration process. The wallet application can choose not to use the webview and instead integrate directly with the API.
