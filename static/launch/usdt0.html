The asset with a unified supply arrives on Stellar

USDT0 is now available on the Stellar network. USDT0 connects Stellar to a unified USDT supply, allowing USDT to move across networks without fragmenting supply. USDT0 is a native Stellar asset that works with your existing stack.

Balances move into and out of Stellar without a custodial bridge or a wrapped representation. Fees are LayerZero messaging costs, quoted per route before you send. Receiving USDT0 needs a trustline on the destination account — see requirements and limitations.
